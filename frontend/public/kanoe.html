Why now

Five converging forces

🧠

LLM quality crossed the threshold

Claude 3.5 and GPT-4o are now capable of multi-step travel bookings. 2024 was the year this became true. We moved first.

🎙️

Voice latency is conversational

Deepgram + streaming TTS achieves sub-500ms response. For the first time, voice AI doesn't feel broken to the user.

🎭

Photorealistic avatars are affordable

HeyGen's streaming API brought avatar interaction from $50k custom builds to $49/month. Any travel brand can now afford it.

📱

Traveller expectations have shifted

Post-pandemic travellers expect instant, personalised service. ChatGPT has normalised conversational AI — they now expect it from every brand.

🔌

API ecosystem is mature

Bland.ai for calls. Resend for email. RateHawk for hotels. The building blocks to complete real bookings now exist as affordable APIs.

🏃

No one has claimed this space

Booking.com and Expedia are too large to pivot. AI startups target consumers. The B2B travel infrastructure gap is entirely unclaimed.
