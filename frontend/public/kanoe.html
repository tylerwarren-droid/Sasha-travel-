Why now

Five converging forces

🧠

LLM quality crossed the threshold

Claude 3.5 and GPT-4o are now good enough at multi-step reasoning to handle real-world travel bookings. 2023 was the year this became true. We moved first.

🎙️

Voice latency is now conversational

Deepgram + streaming TTS now achieves sub-500ms response. For the first time, voice AI doesn't feel broken. This enables a genuinely useful voice concierge.

🎭

Photorealistic avatars are affordable

HeyGen's streaming API brought avatar interaction from $50k custom builds to $49/month. The "AI human" experience is now accessible to any travel brand.

📱

Traveller expectations have shifted

Post-pandemic travellers expect instant, personalised service. ChatGPT has normalised conversational AI. They now expect it from every travel brand they interact with.

🔌

API ecosystem is mature

Bland.ai for outbound calls. Resend for email. RateHawk for hotels. The building blocks to actually complete bookings now exist as affordable APIs.

🏃

No one has claimed this space

Booking.com and Expedia are too large to pivot. AI startups are attacking consumer apps. The B2B travel infrastructure gap remains entirely unclaimed.
