# EspañaMe: re-scoped for the Spanish citizen (B2G)

*EU session, 5 Oct 2026 (EU 160). Read-only, and nothing is built here.*

**Marks:**
- ● read at the official source today;
- ○ a search summary only;
- ✅ built (cited);
- ○ concept.

**The rule throughout is S-77's: Sasha prepares, the citizen presses.** She never signs in with the citizen's Cl@ve,
certificate or DNIe; never submits; never books on a public booking app; never holds credentials.

## 0. The re-scope in one paragraph

**EspañaMe is for a Spanish citizen getting Spanish public services done:** renewing a DNI, a vida laboral, the
renta, a doctor's appointment, a DGT fine, the IBI, the bono social. **It is sold to public money:** ayuntamientos,
diputaciones, regional digital-inclusion programmes.

**It is not a newcomer or relocation product.** That's RelocateMe.
- The padrón → INSS → health-card **checklist for new arrivals moves to RelocateMe**.
- The **foreign-licence exchange** (DGT) moves to RelocateMe.
- EspañaMe keeps **padrón cambio de domicilio**, **DGT renewals, transfers and fines**, and **the SERMAS
  appointment**, all framed for someone already in the system.

**The buyer line** (replacing *"we make citizens arrive complete"*, which was a residence-application line):
> **"Every citizen arrives at your counter with the right papers — or doesn't need to come. We never bypass your
> systems."**

**The design consequence:**
- **Cl@ve is the hub, not a step.** Without it, rows 2, 4, 7 and 9 each still have a no-Cl@ve route (SMS, reference
  number, in person, form or post), and **Sasha's job is to find that route for this citizen.**
- **Three sources refuse automated reading: SEPE, CNMC and the DGT sede.** **Two more are unread:** sede.madrid.es and
  bonosocial.gob.es.
- For those, the cards say *"not read"* (as CR 17's cards already do), and their content comes from **a person (the
  agent lane) or the body's permission**, never from a workaround.

**How public bodies buy** (● BOE, LCSP Ley 9/2017, consolidated text updated 9 Apr 2026):

  the threshold (118.2);
- **supersimplificado:** services **< €60,000** (art. 159.6), 10 working days, no solvency proof, formula scoring;
  **not for "prestaciones de carácter intelectual"**;
- **abierto simplificado:** up to **€140,000** (the State) / **€216,000** (other entities) (arts. 159.1(a), 22.1);
- **innovation (CPI):**
  - **consulta preliminar** (art. 115), which is the door;
  - compra precomercial (art. 8);
  - negotiation for innovative solutions (art. 167(b));
  - **asociación para la innovación** (arts. 177–179).

## 3. Build first: three processes

**Score** = citizen pain × what the first buyer (a diputación, ayuntamiento or social services) wants × what's
already built.

| Rank | Process | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | **SERMAS doctor appointment** (row 5) | **✅ Already built** (CR 4: the SERMAS hand-over, 46/46 rehearsals; S-77's legal study). **No Cl@ve needed:** three identifiers, so it works for the least digital citizens. It's the demo that proves "prepares, you press" on a real public system today. ⚠ Not stored until the DPIA, as now. **Re-frame** it from "new in Madrid" to "your appointment" |
| **2** | **Help: IMV + bono social** (row 9) | **Highest citizen pain, and the most obvious buyer interest:** **social services and digital-inclusion technicians** spend their days on exactly this. Both have **no-certificate routes** (the IMV form or post; the bono social by email or post), so Sasha can carry it to the signature. **The chasing is the product:** the 30-day change reporting, the annual renta, the bono social renewal. ⚠ Bono social: bonosocial.gob.es and cnmc.es are **unread** (CNMC refuses us), so **the 25 %/40 % on madrid.es must be re-read at the BOE (RD 897/2017 consolidated) before any figure is shown** |
| **3** | **The ayuntamiento's own processes: padrón change of address + IBI** (rows 7, 8) | **The first buyer pays for its own counters.** "Fewer padrón queues, IBI paid on time" is the metric a municipal buyer reports. The IBI is **payable without identification** (reference + NIF), so Sasha prepares it end to end and the citizen presses. **The IBI window is open now, to 30 Nov.** ⚠ sede.madrid.es is unread (robots 403), so for a pilot, **read the pilot municipality's own sede**, not Madrid's |

**Next, after the three:**
- **DNI/passport renewal** (row 1): a high-volume, simple, no-Cl@ve cita; the 180-day renewal reminder is pure value.
- **DGT** (row 6): the 20-day fine discount, the transfer's 30 days, and the fraud warning. **Blocked on the sede being
  unread.**
- **The renta** (row 4): the next campaign is spring 2027.

⛔ **SEPE stays at the bottom:** its main site refuses Claude by name. Reading the sede against that intent is the
founder's call (⚖), and the default is no.
