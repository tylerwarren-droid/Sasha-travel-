# Sasha 329 · CR 161: the person's answer goes back as `choices`, then the platform cancel

Tyler, 3 Nov: "do all of that, then the cancel capability".

AgAPI CR 161 (v1.58.0, commits 4e89ed0 and 6d923ee, contract in `agapi_service/tests/test_cr161.py`) finished the platform loop:
- `venue.submit_booking` returns `ask {choice, label, options}` when the platform's form asks the person something;
- `venue.submit_booking` takes `choices {<ask.choice>: <option>}` on the SAME `prepared_id`, and reads it back
  ("…; zona: Salón Interior.");
- `venue.cancel_booking` cancels a beta person's platform booking on the venue's own cancel page, after a read-back and the person's yes
  (booking-rules/2.6 `decide_cancel` is the ONE rule);
- `venue.booking_status` for that act then says `cancelled`.

## Root test (written before the code)

### ONE trip record

The ONE booking record is **AgAPI's act** (the booking on AgAPI's record, `venue_requests` / `venue_sent`, read by
`venue.bookings` and `venue.booking_status`). Sasha keeps a pointer list of the acts it made (`s2_memory` kind `venue_acts`,
`venue_submit._remember`), never a second state:
- **My bookings** reads the act: its state comes from `venue.booking_status` (`venue_submit.bookings`).
- **The person's answer** ("zona: Salón Interior") lives on AgAPI's prepared booking (`venue_prepared.data.choices`), which
  `venue.submit_booking` writes when Sasha sends `choices`. Sasha keeps what was chosen beside the act only to SAY it
  ("…, Salón Interior — reference …"), as the act's `chosen` pointer.
- **Cancelled** is the act's state on AgAPI's record (`venue.booking_status` → `cancelled`). The cancel act Sasha remembers
  (`cancels: <act_id>`, state `cancelled`) is AgAPI's own cancel act (`cancel_act_id`); My bookings reads the booking it cancels as
  cancelled from it, and from `venue.booking_status`, which say the same.

### ONE AgAPI path

- **Book:** `venue.prepare_booking` (`VS.prepare`, once) → `venue.submit_booking` read-back → the person's yes → `venue.submit_booking`.
- **The answer to the platform's question** goes down the SAME path, on the SAME prepared booking: `venue.submit_booking
  {prepared_id, choices}` (its read-back, now with the choice line) → their yes → `venue.submit_booking {prepared_id, choices}`.
  `venue.prepare_booking` is NOT called again. Sasha 328 re-prepared with `answers` — that path is DELETED, and `VS.prepare` no longer
  takes `answers`.
- **Cancel:** `cancel_venue` → `_cancel_act` → `VS.cancel_read_back` (`venue.cancel_booking`, no approval → its read-back) → their
  yes → `VS.cancel_submit` (`venue.cancel_booking` with the approval: the in-app tap, their verified WhatsApp, or the sandbox's
  approval). This is the cancel path every AgAPI act already uses (Sasha 256 / 314 / 317). Sasha 328's link-only `platform_cancel`
  is DELETED: a platform act goes down the same path, and AgAPI decides (`decide_cancel`) whether it presses their cancel or hands
  the link over.

### Second copies: PATCH

**PATCH: Sasha's copy of booking-rules (now 2.6).** `backend/agapi/booking_rules/{booking_rules.py, vectors.json,
test_booking_rules.py, SHA256SUMS}` are byte-identical copies of AgAPI origin/main's `agapi_service/booking_rules/` (sha256 checked
against AgAPI's `SHA256SUMS`; pinned in `tests/test_s265.py` `ROW_965`). It is a SECOND COPY of AgAPI's ONE rule.
- **Why it is still here.** Sasha decides locally which question to ask before AgAPI is called (`booking_decide`, `rules_for`), and
  the AgAPI op that would return a decision for a given context does not exist yet. Removing the copy means every turn waits on AgAPI.
- **What holds it.** The hashes are pinned (`test_s265`), and AgAPI's own vectors run against it (`test_booking_rules.py`).
- **Follow-up.** Read the rules from AgAPI (an op that returns `decide`'s answer, or AgAPI's package) and delete the copy.

**No other second copy.** The answer is not re-prepared, the cancel link is not a second cancel path, the act's state is not
re-derived.

### The walk's sentence: removed, not kept as a fallback

Sasha 328's PATCH b (`venue_submit.choice_of`, a regex over AgAPI's sentence "Their booking asks for {name}: …. Which one?") is
DELETED, with no fallback. Why:
- In AgAPI origin/main, the walk's only question stop (`platform_form.walk`, `Stop("choice", …, ask={choice, label, options})`)
  always carries `ask`, and `venue_submit._send_platform` / `submit` always put it on the result. Every path that asks has `ask`.
- An AgAPI without `ask` (before CR 161) also does not take `choices` on `venue.submit_booking`. A question read from its sentence
  could not be answered there, so asking it would be a loop. Such a stop is said as the stop it is, with their page.

### The tap-as-yes rule (Sasha 328, kept)

The tap (or their words naming exactly ONE of AgAPI's options) is the person's yes only when ALL hold:
- it comes from the app (`VS.TAP`) or their verified WhatsApp (`VS.WA`);
- the new read-back is exactly what they said yes to PLUS the choice: AgAPI's lines with the `; zona: Salón Interior` they chose
  taken out equal (`_same_words`) the lines they said yes to, and the choice line is there;
- the claim succeeds, once (`claim_words`).

Otherwise the new read-back (with the choice line) is shown, and their next yes sends it.

### The cancel's yes

A platform cancel presses a button on a third party's page, which cannot be undone. AgAPI reads it back
("Kanoe opens La Taberna's own cancellation on CoverManager and presses its cancel for your booking …"). Sasha shows that read-back
with one line, "Cancel La Taberna, Fri 20 Nov 22:00, 2 people, ref SBX-ABC123?", and sends only on their yes in a later turn
(`explicit_yes` + `claim`). Sasha 314's one-instruction rule ("cancel my DiverXO booking" from the app IS the approval) does NOT apply
to a platform act: the read-back is always shown first.

## What is built

1. **The question, from `ask`.** `_platform_result` reads `r["ask"]` (only when the walk failed). It asks once, with AgAPI's options as
   taps (`pick:<option>`, exactly AgAPI's) and AgAPI's label. The same card, the same WhatsApp buttons (Sasha 328), no frontend change.
2. **The answer.** `platform_answer` → `VS.read_back(prepared_id, choices={ask.choice: option})` → AgAPI's read-back with the choice
   line → the tap-as-yes rule → `_finish` → `VS.submit` (the in-app tap on that read-back; with an approval, the body again carries
   `choices` and the same `prepared_id`).
3. **Booked.** "✅ Booked: La Taberna, …, Salón Interior — reference …", and My bookings shows the act with its reference and what was
   chosen.
4. **Cancel.** "Cancel La Taberna" / "cancel it" → AgAPI's read-back + "Cancel La Taberna, Fri 20 Nov 22:00, 2 people, ref …?" →
   "yes" → `venue.cancel_booking` with the approval →
   - `cancelled`: "Cancelled — La Taberna, Fri 20 Nov 22:00, 2 people, ref …. Their page says “Reserva anulada”." My bookings
     reads it cancelled.
   - `not_allowed` (a fee, a security check, their page not naming the booking, a sign-in, outside the beta): AgAPI's own words (why),
     their cancel link as the button (AgAPI's `takeover` / `link`), and the venue's phone when its card shows one. Nothing was cancelled.
